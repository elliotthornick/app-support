# Support — Elliott Hornick apps
Need help with **iControl Snap**, **RPR: Roll Punch Repeat**, or **Gassy Capys**?
Email: joshhornick@gmail.com — please include your app name, iPhone model, and iOS version.

Quick answers:
- **Restore a purchase:** in the app, use "Restore Purchases" (App Store purchases are tied to your Apple ID).
- **Promo code not working:** codes are one-time use and device-bound; check for typos (the code format is VADER-XXXX-XXXX).
- **Ads still showing after unlock:** force-quit and reopen the app, then tap Restore Purchases.
